Our Story

Manzil Bharat was born from a simple idea: travel across India should be seamless, affordable, and accessible to everyone. We started as a small team of technology enthusiasts determined to solve real-world travel problems through innovation.

Today, Manzil Bharat is building an AI-powered platform that connects travelers with cabs, bikes, flights, trains, buses, hotels, restaurants, and local services — all in one place.
