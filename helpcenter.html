Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a service on ManzilBharat?

Browse available services on our platform, select your preferred option, enter your travel details, and complete the booking. You'll receive a confirmation via email and SMS.

How do I cancel my booking?

You can cancel your booking through the platform or by contacting our support team. Refund eligibility depends on the cancellation policy of the service provider. Visit our Refund & Cancellation Policy for details.

When will I receive my refund?

Once approved, refunds are typically processed within 5-10 business days. The time for the amount to appear in your account depends on your bank or payment provider.

How do I modify my booking?

Contact our support team with your booking reference number. Modifications are subject to availability and the service provider's policy.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes. Payments are processed through authorized and secure payment gateways. ManzilBharat does not store your card details. Read our Cookie Policy and Privacy Policy for more information.

How do I contact customer support?

You can reach us via email at support@manzilbharat.in or call us at +91 941 104 5187. Our team is available Mon-Sat, 9:00 AM - 8:00 PM.

How do I create an account?

Click "Create Account" on the homepage, enter your details, and verify your email or phone number. You can then start booking services immediately.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, and other popular payment methods through our secure payment partners.
