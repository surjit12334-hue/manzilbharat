Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: September 2026
Last Updated: September 2026

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains the conditions under which refunds may be provided for bookings and services made through ManzilBharat.

This Policy is intended to comply with applicable laws and regulations of India, including applicable consumer-protection and e-commerce requirements.

1. Scope

ManzilBharat is a tourism platform connecting users with hotels, transportation providers, tour operators, guides, attractions, restaurants, and other service providers.

Refund eligibility may depend on:

  • The type of service booked
  • The cancellation time
  • The cancellation policy of the service provider
  • The reason for cancellation
  • Applicable taxes and charges
  • Applicable Indian laws and regulations

2. Cancellation Before Booking

Users may cancel or modify a booking before completing payment without being charged, unless otherwise clearly stated before confirmation.

Once payment has been successfully completed, the cancellation terms applicable to that particular service will apply.

3. Refund for User Cancellation

If a user cancels a booking, the refund will be determined according to the cancellation policy displayed before or at the time of booking.

Possible outcomes include:

  • Full refund: When cancellation is made within the eligible free-cancellation period.
  • Partial refund: When the applicable service provider permits a partial refund.
  • No refund: When cancellation occurs after the applicable cancellation deadline or the booking is explicitly marked non-refundable.

Any applicable cancellation charges will be clearly communicated wherever reasonably possible.

4. Non-Refundable Bookings

Some hotels, tickets, activities, transportation services, and promotional offers may be marked as non-refundable.

For such bookings, cancellation may not result in a refund except where required by applicable law or where the relevant service provider voluntarily permits a refund.

Users should carefully review the booking conditions before making payment.

5. Cancellation by Hotel or Service Provider

If a hotel, transport provider, tour operator, attraction, or other service provider cancels a confirmed booking and the user is entitled to a refund, ManzilBharat will facilitate the refund process where the payment was processed through ManzilBharat or its authorized payment partner.

Where possible, ManzilBharat may offer an alternative service with the user's consent.

6. Cancellation by ManzilBharat

ManzilBharat may cancel a transaction in circumstances including:

  • Fraudulent or suspicious activity
  • Incorrect pricing caused by a technical error
  • Unavailability of the service
  • Violation of platform terms
  • Circumstances beyond reasonable control
  • Legal or regulatory requirements

Where a refund is applicable, eligible amounts will be returned through the original payment method or another lawful method.

7. Travel Disruptions and Force Majeure

Refunds arising from events such as natural disasters, severe weather, government restrictions, strikes, pandemics, civil disturbances, transportation disruptions, or other circumstances beyond reasonable control will depend on the applicable service provider's policy and applicable law.

ManzilBharat will reasonably assist users in communicating with the relevant service provider.

8. Refund Processing Time

Once a refund has been approved, ManzilBharat or its payment partner will initiate the refund within the applicable processing period.

The time taken for the amount to appear in the user's bank account, card, UPI account, or other payment method may depend on the relevant bank, payment provider, or financial institution.

9. Payment Gateway Charges

Where applicable, payment gateway, transaction, convenience, or other charges may be non-refundable if they represent actual charges incurred for processing the transaction, subject to applicable law and the terms disclosed to the user.

No charge will be retained where retention is prohibited by applicable law.

10. Duplicate Payments

If a user is charged more than once for the same transaction due to a technical or payment-processing error, the duplicate amount will be eligible for refund after verification.

Users should report duplicate transactions to ManzilBharat as soon as possible.

11. Failed Transactions

If money has been deducted from a user's account but the booking has not been successfully confirmed, ManzilBharat will investigate the transaction.

Where the transaction has failed and the amount is received by ManzilBharat or its payment partner, the eligible amount will be refunded according to the applicable payment-processing procedures.

12. Refund Method

Unless otherwise required by law, approved refunds will normally be processed through the original payment method used for the transaction.

For example:

  • UPI payment → refund through the applicable payment channel
  • Card payment → refund to the relevant card/payment method
  • Other payment methods → refund according to the applicable payment provider's process

13. How to Request a Refund

Users can submit a refund or cancellation request through the ManzilBharat platform or by contacting customer support.

The request may require:

  • Booking/reference number
  • Registered contact details
  • Reason for cancellation/refund
  • Relevant transaction information

Users should not share passwords, PINs, OTPs, CVVs, or other authentication credentials when requesting a refund.

14. Refund Disputes

If a user believes that a refund has been incorrectly denied or calculated, they may contact ManzilBharat's customer-support or grievance-redressal team.

ManzilBharat will review the request and applicable booking terms.

Where a dispute concerns a third-party service provider, ManzilBharat may coordinate with that provider to resolve the matter.

15. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this Policy is intended to restrict or remove any rights or remedies available to consumers under applicable Indian law.

Where applicable, users may exercise their rights under Indian consumer-protection laws and other applicable regulations.

16. Changes to the Refund Policy

ManzilBharat may update this Refund & Cancellation Policy when necessary due to changes in its services, business practices, or applicable laws.

The updated policy will be published on the ManzilBharat platform with a revised "Last Updated" date.
