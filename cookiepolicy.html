Cookie Policy

Effective Date: September 2026
Last Updated: September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how ManzilBharat uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit or use our website, mobile application, and related services.

This Policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit, improve functionality, provide security, and understand how users interact with the platform.

ManzilBharat may also use similar technologies such as pixels, local storage, SDKs, and device identifiers where applicable.

2. Why ManzilBharat Uses Cookies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Keep users securely logged in.
  • Remember language and travel preferences.
  • Maintain booking and session information.
  • Improve website and application performance.
  • Understand how users interact with our platform.
  • Detect fraud and security threats.
  • Personalize travel recommendations.
  • Measure the effectiveness of our services.
  • Provide relevant communications or advertising where applicable.

3. Types of Cookies We May Use

A. Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for essential platform functions.

They may be used for:

  • Login and authentication
  • Account security
  • Booking functionality
  • Session management
  • Fraud prevention
  • Platform security

These cookies generally cannot be disabled without affecting essential functionality.

B. Preference Cookies

These cookies remember choices made by users, such as:

  • Language
  • Currency
  • Location preferences
  • Search preferences
  • Display settings

They help provide a more personalized experience.

C. Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors use ManzilBharat.

For example, they may help us understand:

  • Which pages are visited most frequently
  • How users navigate the platform
  • Which features are being used
  • General platform performance

Where required by applicable law, these cookies will only be used after obtaining appropriate consent.

D. Personalization Cookies

These cookies may help ManzilBharat provide personalized travel recommendations based on your interactions with the platform.

Examples may include:

  • Destination recommendations
  • Hotel suggestions
  • Tourism content
  • Activity recommendations

E. Advertising Cookies

If ManzilBharat uses advertising or marketing technologies, cookies may be used to measure advertising performance or display relevant advertisements.

Where consent is required, we will request consent before placing or using such cookies.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some features of ManzilBharat may use services provided by third parties.

These may include:

  • Analytics providers
  • Map and navigation providers
  • Payment providers
  • Authentication providers
  • Advertising or marketing providers
  • Security providers

Third parties may place or access cookies according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

ManzilBharat does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party providers.

5. Managing Cookie Preferences

You may manage or disable cookies through:

  • The cookie-consent settings provided on ManzilBharat.
  • Your browser settings.
  • Your device settings.
  • Third-party privacy controls where available.

Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the platform, including login, booking, personalization, or other services.

6. Cookie Consent

Where applicable, ManzilBharat will provide users with appropriate information and choices regarding non-essential cookies.

Users may be able to:

  • Accept cookies.
  • Reject non-essential cookies.
  • Customize cookie preferences.
  • Withdraw previously provided consent.

Withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing that occurred before withdrawal.

7. Cookies and Personal Data

Some cookies may collect information that can be associated with an identifiable user or device.

Where cookie-related information constitutes personal data under applicable Indian law, ManzilBharat will process it in accordance with applicable data-protection requirements and our Privacy Policy.

8. Security Cookies

Certain cookies and similar technologies may be used to protect users and ManzilBharat from:

  • Unauthorized access
  • Account abuse
  • Fraud
  • Automated attacks
  • Suspicious activity

These technologies may be necessary to maintain the security of the platform.

9. Cookie Retention

Cookies may be either:

Session Cookies: Deleted when you close your browser or session.

Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device for a defined period or until they are manually deleted.

The retention period may vary depending on the purpose of the cookie.

10. Changes to This Cookie Policy

ManzilBharat may update this Cookie Policy from time to time because of changes in technology, platform features, third-party services, or applicable laws.

Any updated version will be published on the ManzilBharat platform with a revised "Last Updated" date.
