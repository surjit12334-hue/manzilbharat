Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026
Last Updated: September 2026

ManzilBharat ("ManzilBharat", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect information when you use the ManzilBharat website, mobile application, and related tourism services.

This Policy is intended to operate in accordance with applicable laws of India, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and applicable rules and regulations made under it.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use ManzilBharat, we may collect:

A. Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number
  • Login credentials or authentication information
  • Profile information

B. Travel Information

  • Travel destinations
  • Travel dates
  • Preferred activities
  • Budget preferences
  • Accommodation preferences
  • Food and travel preferences
  • Itinerary information

C. Location Information

With your permission, we may process location information to provide features such as:

  • Nearby hotels and attractions
  • Navigation and route recommendations
  • Local businesses
  • Location-based travel suggestions
  • Safety and emergency features

You may control location permissions through your device settings.

D. Booking and Transaction Information

If booking or payment services are provided through ManzilBharat, we may process information necessary to complete the transaction, such as booking details and transaction identifiers.

Payment credentials may be processed by authorized payment service providers rather than being stored directly by ManzilBharat, where applicable.

E. Technical Information

We may automatically receive limited technical information such as:

  • IP address
  • Device type
  • Browser or application information
  • Operating system
  • Log information
  • Approximate usage information

We use this information primarily for security, troubleshooting, analytics, and improving our services.

2. Why We Use Your Data

We may process personal data for clearly specified purposes, including:

  • Creating and managing your account
  • Providing tourism and travel services
  • Generating personalized itineraries
  • Recommending hotels, attractions, restaurants and local businesses
  • Processing bookings and transactions
  • Providing location-based services where enabled
  • Providing customer support
  • Improving platform functionality
  • Detecting fraud, abuse, and security threats
  • Maintaining platform security
  • Communicating important service-related information
  • Complying with legal obligations

We will seek consent where consent is required as the legal basis for processing.

3. Consent

Where processing is based on consent, ManzilBharat will seek consent through a clear and understandable mechanism.

Consent should be freely given, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous, with clear affirmative action, as required by the applicable DPDP framework.

You may withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent. The withdrawal process should be as easy as the process used to provide consent.

Withdrawal of consent may affect our ability to provide certain services that depend on that data.

4. Children's Data

ManzilBharat takes children's privacy seriously.

Where applicable under Indian law, ManzilBharat will obtain the required consent from a parent or lawful guardian before processing a child's personal data and will comply with applicable requirements concerning children's data.

We will not knowingly use children's personal data for prohibited activities.

We may share necessary information with:

  • Hotels and accommodation providers

  • Local guides and tourism businesses
  • Payment and transaction service providers
  • Cloud hosting and technology providers

We will seek to share only information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

Third-party service providers receiving personal data are expected to process it in accordance with applicable contractual and legal requirements.

6. AI and Personalized Recommendations

ManzilBharat may use artificial intelligence and automated systems to generate:

  • Travel itineraries

  • Budget suggestions

  • Improve user experience

ManzilBharat will implement reasonable technical and organisational security measures designed to protect personal data from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction.

We will retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected or as required or permitted by applicable law.
